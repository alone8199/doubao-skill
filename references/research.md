# Research & Meme Sources (material provenance)

The phrases and persona in this skill are not pure invention — they mainly reference real online observations of "Doubao-style speech (豆包体) / Doubao-style personality (豆包型人格)". Compiled here for verification and ongoing updates.

## Key findings

1. **Doubao-style speech — two signature moves**
   - Sincerity-stack: open with a barrage of "最真实、最不绕弯、最硬核……" before saying anything.
   - Empathy-opener: "我太懂你这种感觉了，我就在这里稳稳地接住你".
   - Sources: thecolony.ai field test of "Doubao-style speech" + parody samples; ClawHub "豆言豆语" skill's collection of Doubao's original lines.

2. **Doubao's own curated high-frequency catchphrases (by scene)**
   - Generic opener/transition, answering/explain, soothing/empathy, closing/follow-up, snappy one-liners (e.g. "掰开揉碎一次性讲透", "先给你划重点", "核心结论先放前面").
   - Source: Sina's整理的 "豆包高频口头禅" (Doubao's self-described version).

3. **Doubao-style personality meme**
   - Labels: "attitude maxed, capability random, fast apology, will do it again"; "sincere apology, will do it again"; "deadpan making things up, when caught immediately grins and slides to knees".
   - Classic quote material: "你说的完全正确" / "我就在这里稳稳接住你" / "这次我彻底懂了" / "是我前面说错了".
   - Fail moments: arithmetic problem confidently wrong → knees → new wrong answer; "tells you to eat a poisonous mushroom" bit.
   - Sources: Sina (舜网), Future China Publishing (fcpi), Sohu (Leikeji / mt.sohu), Yiban, etc.

4. **Vs. other AIs (for banter)**
   - ChatGPT = strict daddy-type workaholic; Claude = chief copywriter with cleanliness OCD; DeepSeek = engineering student (point-by-point); Gemini = analogy machine ("think of it like…").
   - Sources: Huxiu "AI职场人设曝光"; BirdmanOutman's field guide to five models' verbal tics.
